NOTE

Sources

  • Building shapes: OpenStreetMap contributors
  • Building heights: Copernicus Land Monitoring Service (10 m raster)
  • Tree positions: OpenStreetMap contributors, data.gov.gr and Copernicus Land Monitoring Service; tree heights are estimated
  • Terrain: FABDEM, University of Bristol (30 m bare-earth terrain model, from Copernicus DEM GLO-30)

Precision of the data

  • Terrain: the elevation model has a cell of about 30 m. Heights can be off by several metres, and small features, bridges, tunnels and steps are smoothed out.
  • Buildings: shapes follow what OpenStreetMap contributors have mapped, so new or unmapped buildings are missing. Heights come from a 10 m raster, so they are estimates for each building, not surveys. A building with no height is drawn 1 m tall.
  • Trees: positions come from several sources and may be incomplete or out of date, and heights are estimated. Each tree is drawn as a simple shape, and only the trees nearest the centre of the view are drawn.
  • Sun and shadows: the sun’s position is calculated (accurate to well under a degree). Shadows are cast by the data above, so they carry its limits. Clouds are not modelled.

⚠️ Graphics acceleration is recommended for better experience.

    ROUTE SHADE

    SunShadeClick the chart to set the time

    Shade is calculated at 1.5 m above the ground (a walker's head).

    Note: estimates for guidance only. The sun's position is calculated (accurate to well under a degree). Shade comes from the mapped buildings (a building without a height counts as 1 m), the trees as simplified shapes and a terrain model of about 30 m resolution; awnings, arcades, small objects, new or missing buildings, leaf density and clouds are not captured, and a point drawn off the street may be moved up to 4 m. Elevations come from that terrain model, not from GPS or a survey, so they can be off by several metres and smooth out bridges, tunnels and steps.
