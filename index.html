Welcome to Athens GIS Repository

Here you can explore information about the administrative boundaries, transportation, environment, and population of the Athens Metropolitan Area.

Use the panel on the left side of the map to explore the layers you’re interested in, then click the button to download them. You can find more details about the selected layers at the bottom left of the map.

Have fun!
